## Reading this run

Windows 11 host, natively, while other test suites ran on the same machine and kept thousands
of loopback connections in TIME_WAIT. The errors (EOF and `Only one usage of each socket
address`) are ephemeral-port exhaustion: each idle connection takes two ports and REALITY one
more for its target, out of 16384, held 120 s after close. Steps that did not reach their
target and scenarios that failed their warm-up are not comparable. The connection-rate phase
is off (`-rate auto` on Windows). QUIC (hysteria2, tuic-v5) at 20-110 MB/s is Windows UDP
without GSO/GRO, not Citrus. Use `2026-10-13-docker-linux.md` for the baseline and the
optimization list; this file is for comparison with later Windows runs on a quiet machine.
What agrees with Linux: live heap per idle connection (43-55 KB, 187 KB for Shadowsocks 2022,
+31-32 KB with a user limit), goroutines per connection, and the throughput ranking.
