The roadmap covers every getmikan repository: the [Citrus](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus) core and the [utls](https://github.com/getmikan/utls), [tls](https://github.com/getmikan/tls), [http](https://github.com/getmikan/http), [smux](https://github.com/getmikan/smux) and [restls-client-go](https://github.com/getmikan/restls-client-go) forks. Moving the [Mikan](https://github.com/Miroshka000/mikan) panel to Citrus depends on the core, so it is here too. This is a plan, not a schedule.

## Done

The details, with a link to every PR, are in the [changelog](/Citrus/en/changelog/).

- **Releases.** The beta releases [`v1.0.0-beta.1`](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/releases/tag/v1.0.0-beta.1) and [`v1.0.0-beta.2`](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/releases/tag/v1.0.0-beta.2), built from `dev`; see [the second beta's news post](/Citrus/en/news/beta-2/).
- **Xray compatibility.** Post-quantum REALITY on the client and the server (X25519MLKEM768, ML-DSA-65), XHTTP with Xray's behaviour, ECH keys and links in Xray's formats. Automated tests against Xray v26.10.10 in CI.
- **Users in the core.** Multi-user Shadowsocks 2022, traffic and online-address stats, limits on speed, connections, addresses, quota and expiry, inbound users changed without a restart, `PUT /rules`.
- **Events and observability.** `GET /events` (WebSocket, SSE, NDJSON), Prometheus `/metrics`, `/healthz` and `/readyz` probes, rejected-authentication events, JSON logs.
- **Cascades.** `type: chain` groups with a check of every hop.
- **Networking and DNS.** Listeners on a unix socket, DNSCrypt v2, a BitTorrent sniffer and the `PROTOCOL` rule, `trusted-proxy-header` from trusted proxies.
- **Cleanup.** VMess, SSR, Hysteria v1, TUIC v4, Shadowsocks stream ciphers, v2ray-plugin and mKCP removed.
- **Hardening.** A security review, a code review and a static audit, all closed with fixes, including the remaining findings on server handshakes, fallback, 0-RTT replay and the utls and ResTLS forks ([#140](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/140)–[#142](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/142), [#148](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/148)); TLS 1.2 minimum; self-update checked against `checksums.txt`; CI pinned by digest. See [Security](/Citrus/en/security/).
- **Performance.** Upload: write batching, up to +50% on REALITY, Trojan and SS2022 ([#119](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/119)). Download: 64 KiB reads and TLS records sent to the socket together, with 32 streams REALITY 2802 → 4235 MB/s, Trojan 2737 → 3936, SS2022 3895 → 4392 ([#124](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/124)). Latency under load: an echo next to eight downloads 55–63 → 20 ms ([#131](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/131)). Idle tunnel memory: Trojan −36%, AnyTLS −18%, REALITY −12% ([#129](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/129)). QUIC: the gap to sing-box on new streams 10–12% → 4–6% ([#125](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/125)); fewer allocations per new connection ([#118](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/118)). Earlier: XHTTP over h2 uses half the CPU per gigabyte, an idle connection takes 30 KB less memory.
- **AnyTLS.** A rework ([#130](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/130)): with 32 streams download is twice as fast, upload +23%, ahead of sing-box; the client race is gone.
- **For servers and panels.** PROXY protocol v1 and v2 on the TCP inbounds for front ends such as an SNI router ([#136](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/136)); non-root systemd and Docker profiles ([#144](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/144)); one-time tickets for controller WebSockets instead of the secret in the URL ([#143](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/143)); the User-Agent `clash.meta/<version> citrus/<version>` ([#146](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/146)) and a `core` field in `GET /version` ([#147](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/147)), so panels and apps tell Citrus from mihomo.
- **Comparison and measurements.** A comparison of four servers, Xray, sing-box, mihomo and Citrus, on eight protocols ([#126](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/126)); a nightly performance check in CI against mihomo ([#127](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/127)).
- **Client compatibility.** Automated tests: mihomo v1.19.31 and v1.19.32 clients against a Citrus server on every protocol, see [Compatibility](/Citrus/en/compatibility/).
- **Go 1.26.** The minimum Go version is 1.26, so `golang.org/x/net`, `x/crypto` and `x/text` are on current releases with their security fixes. The separate builds on old Go versions (`-go120` to `-go125`) are gone; see [Migrating from mihomo](/Citrus/en/migration/#platforms-and-builds).
- **Forks.** utls `v1.8.8-citrus.1`–`.3`, tls `v0.1.8-citrus.1`, http `v0.1.8-citrus.1`–`.2`; utls and tls have `dev` commits after these tags (download, memory). The new smux fork stops a caller's buffer from being sent after an error, and the restls-client-go fork fixes its record-counter races; neither has a tag yet.
- **Desync outbound.** `type: desync` is a DIRECT connection for networks with DPI filtering: it changes how the first TLS ClientHello or QUIC Initial is sent, not what the server receives. Splits and reordering, out-of-band data, fakes with an automatic TTL, waiting for the send to be acknowledged, ClientHello and QUIC decoys; what the OS cannot do, the core reports instead of skipping it silently. Built on the [desync](https://github.com/Nemu-x/desync) library v0.2, it works on Windows, Linux, macOS and Android ([#163](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/163), [#172](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/172), [#180](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/180)); see [Desync outbound](/Citrus/en/start/desync/).
- **Fallbacks as in Xray.** VLESS and Trojan hand streams that are not theirs to a fallback by SNI, ALPN and path ([#159](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/159)), with a `fallback-page` for ws, grpc and xhttp ([#153](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/153)); see [VLESS and Trojan fallbacks](/Citrus/en/start/fallbacks/). XHTTP over HTTP/3 on the VLESS inbound ([#160](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/160)).
- **The server stays out of places it should not reach.** `egress-guard` keeps inbound users off private, local and special addresses, cloud metadata included ([#162](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/162)); see [Server without root](/Citrus/en/start/server-profile/). Root certificates: `tls.ca-roots` picks the source, and CAs used for state TLS interception are denied by default ([#161](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/161)); see [Security](/Citrus/en/security/#root-certificates).
- **Speed groups.** `speed-group` shares one speed limit between several users, with `SetLimits` and `OnLeave` in the Go API ([#151](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/151)); see [User limits](/Citrus/en/start/user-limits/).
- **A second audit of the whole core.** Networking and DNS, adapters, inbounds, TUN and the dialer, transports, rules and the tunnel, updates and the controller; every finding is closed with a fix ([#166](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/166)–[#171](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/171), [#176](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/176)–[#178](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/178)). Among them: self-update replaces the core atomically and rolls back on failure, Mux.Cool sessions are isolated, and a failed inbound reload is rolled back. On Windows the core reports when Forwarding or WeakHostSend is on for the TUN egress interface ([#157](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/157)).
- **More automated tests.** sing-box client and server against Citrus ([#158](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/158)), the whole inbound test suite under the race detector ([#150](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/150)).
- **MikanApp on Citrus.** The app is built with the Citrus core; its About screen names the core after the core itself — Citrus or Mihomo.
- **The mikan panel on Citrus.** The panel's `go.mod` replaces the core with Citrus, and utls, tls, http, smux and restls-client-go with the getmikan forks; VMess is gone from the panel.
- **Documentation.** The site in two languages: getting started, configuration, controller, users, limits, metrics, events, chains, fallbacks, desync, the server profile, migrating from mihomo.

## In progress

- **Release `v1.0.0-beta.3`.** Everything done since the second beta is only in `dev` so far. Before the release: the changelog for [#149](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/149)–[#180](https://github.com/getmikan/Citrus/pull/180).
- **Sync from mihomo.** 17 new mihomo commits conflict with Citrus in ten files (Sudoku, Shadowsocks, `go.mod`, the link converter, `docs/config.yaml`) and are being merged by hand. The weekly workflow cannot report this itself: the organization does not let GitHub Actions open pull requests or issues.
- **The mikan panel on Citrus: what is left.**
  - PQ REALITY in the panel's inbounds (`mldsa65-seed`, `require-x25519mlkem768`) and `pqv=` in subscription links;
  - REALITY's `max-time-difference` is now in milliseconds — check what the panel writes;
  - the panel's e2e and slice tests on a Citrus image instead of mihomo v1.19.31.
- **A live check.** A test node on Citrus and a phone checklist: connecting with every protocol in the subscription, network switches and airplane mode, speed, stats and limits in the panel.
- **English docs on par with Russian.** The site pages exist in both languages; next are the comments in `docs/config.yaml`, which are still mostly in Chinese, as in mihomo.

## Next

- **Shadowsocks 2022 performance.** Every connection currently allocates two 64 KB buffers up front; in all, sing-shadowsocks allocates about 150 KiB per connection in advance. The plan is a sing-shadowsocks fork that takes buffers only while data flows.
- **Release signing.** Sign `checksums.txt` in CI and verify the signature with a key built into Citrus on self-update.
- **Verify downloaded dashboards and geodata.**
- **Further hardening.** More reviews and audits as the code grows.

## For the mikan panel and apps

Tasks from the mikan development plan that depend on the core. The number in brackets is the task's number in that plan.

- **Paid access after a subscription ends (13).** An expired user gets a limited connection instead of a stub: only Telegram, payment pages and the subscription page, with a small traffic and speed limit. The node must cut everything else, not rules in the client. Citrus has the `IN-USER` rule; it needs checking together with the limits across the whole scenario.
- **A special mode for MikanApp and Kabosu (E2).** The panel recognises its own apps by `citrus/<version>` in the User-Agent and gives them everything Citrus can do: PQ REALITY, protocols mihomo lacks, desync.
- **Desync in MikanApp.** A Desync-only profile, automatic strategy selection per service and desync on top of a subscription already work in the app MikanApp was forked from; bring them over.
- **Citrus for app developers (E4).** Ready builds for embedding: an Android AAR, an iOS xcframework, a desktop library; documentation and a "Powered by Citrus" mark.
- **A Citrus page on the mikan site (19).** What the core can do and how it differs, with links to the measurements and the core comparison.

## Needs a decision

- **A sing-quic fork.** Hysteria2 padding costs about 4% CPU per stream; fixing it needs a fork of sing-quic.
- **The TUIC stream limit.** The TUIC server accepts at most 2048 streams per QUIC connection; a sing-box client, which does not wait for stream credit, gets errors past that.
- **A sing-mux sub-stream limit.** Limit the number of sub-streams in Citrus's own handler without forking sing-mux.
- **Release tags for the forks.** smux and restls-client-go have no `-citrus` tags, and utls and tls each have 11 `dev` commits after their last tag; Citrus takes all four by pseudo-version.
